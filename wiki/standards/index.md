# Standards

ISO/IEC 29147 (disclosure) and ISO/IEC 30111 (handling), ETSI TR 103 838, the FIRST PSIRT Services Framework and CVD guidelines, recorded as identifiers, scope and links.

## Concepts

- [ISO/IEC 29147:2018 Vulnerability Disclosure Standard](iso-iec-29147.md) — International benchmark standard specifying guidelines for vendors and coordinators on receiving, investigating, and disclosing vulnerabilities.
- [ISO/IEC 30111:2019 Vulnerability Handling Processes Standard](iso-iec-30111.md) — International standard specifying internal organizational processes for investigating, triaging, remediating, and fixing vulnerabilities.
