# Summary

**ETSI TR 103 838** provides a comprehensive technical overview and guidance framework for implementing coordinated vulnerability disclosure across European national authorities and industrial sectors[^etsi-tr-103-838].

# Key Technical Focus Areas
1. **Multi-Party Coordination**: Recommends protocols for coordinating vulnerabilities that affect telecommunications and Internet infrastructure across multiple EU jurisdictions.
2. **Safe-Harbour Harmonization**: Analyzes disparities in national cybercrime legislation and recommends harmonized good-faith researcher protections.
3. **CSIRT Capacity Building**: Outlines operational capabilities required for designated CSIRTs acting as national CVD intermediaries.
