# Summary

The **FIRST PSIRT Services Framework** provides a high-level organizational model defining the operational capabilities, services, and activities required for effective Product Security Incident Response Teams (PSIRTs)[^first-psirt-services-framework].

# Six Core Service Areas
1. **Vulnerability Discovery**: Intake channels, research programs, and internal automated scanning.
2. **Vulnerability Triage**: Verification, reproduction, root-cause analysis, and severity scoring.
3. **Remediation Engineering**: Patch design, development, backporting, and security regression testing.
4. **Advisory & Communication**: Drafting CSAF/human advisories and coordinating with external finders.
5. **Stakeholder Collaboration**: Working with external vendors, national CSIRTs, and CNA Roots.
6. **Post-Mortem & Improvement**: Defect escape analysis and secure software development lifecycle improvements.
