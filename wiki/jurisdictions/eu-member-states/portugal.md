# Summary

In Portugal, the **Centro Nacional de Cibersegurança (CNCS)** and its operational arm **CERT.PT** coordinate vulnerability disclosures[^eu-nis2-directive].

# National CVD Framework
- **Designated Coordinator**: CERT.PT acts as national focal point under NIS2 Article 12(1).
- **Good Practices**: CNCS issues national recommendations for coordinated vulnerability disclosure.
- **Safe-Harbour Status**: Compliance with CNCS guidance mitigates risk of cybercrime prosecution under Portuguese law.
