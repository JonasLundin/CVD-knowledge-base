# Summary

In Slovakia, **SK-CERT** (under NBÚ) and **CSIRT.SK** (under the Ministry of Investments) oversee coordinated vulnerability disclosure[^eu-nis2-directive].

# National CVD Framework
- **Designated Coordinator**: SK-CERT manages national critical sector disclosures.
- **CVD Process**: Formal notification forms and structured handling mechanisms.
- **Safe-Harbour Status**: Penal code protections rely on adherence to non-disruptive testing principles and prompt submission.
