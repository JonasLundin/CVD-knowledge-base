# Summary

The Netherlands pioneered European CVD through the **Dutch CVD Guidelines** published by **NCSC-NL** and backed by the Dutch Public Prosecution Service (Openbaar Ministerie)[^eu-nis2-directive].

# National CVD Framework
- **Designated Coordinator**: NCSC-NL acts as national coordinator and trusted intermediary.
- **Prosecution Policy**: The Dutch Public Prosecution Service issued a formal policy letter instructing prosecutors not to indict security researchers who comply with CVD principles (proportionality, subsidiarity, prompt reporting).
- **CVD Guidelines**: Prescribes clear steps for both organizations and finders.
