# Summary

In Hungary, **GovCERT-Hungary**, operated within the National Cyber Defence Institute (**NKI**), serves as the designated national CVD coordinator[^eu-nis2-directive].

# National CVD Framework
- **Designated Coordinator**: GovCERT-Hungary manages vulnerability intake for state institutions and critical services.
- **Legislative Basis**: Hungarian Act L of 2013 on Electronic Information Security, updated for NIS2 conformity.
- **Safe-Harbour Status**: Researchers must report vulnerabilities exclusively through official channels without prior public disclosure.
