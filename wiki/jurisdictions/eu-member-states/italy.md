# Summary

Italy has modernized its coordinated vulnerability disclosure framework under **Decreto Legislativo n. 138/2024**, with **CSIRT Italia** (operating within **ACN**) designated as the national coordinator[^eu-nis2-directive].

# National CVD Framework
- **Designated Coordinator**: CSIRT Italia (Agenzia per la Cybersicurezza Nazionale).
- **National Policy**: ACN maintains official national guidelines on responsible reporting and structured remediation timelines.
- **Safe-Harbour Status**: D.Lgs. 138/2024 establishes procedures shielding bona-fide reporters who follow ACN guidelines from immediate penal prosecution.
