# Summary

Poland distributes national CSIRT functions across three Tier-1 coordinators: **CSIRT NASK**, **CSIRT GOV**, and **CSIRT MON**, with CSIRT NASK leading civilian vulnerability coordination[^eu-nis2-directive].

# National CVD Framework
- **Designated Coordinator**: CSIRT NASK coordinates disclosures for the civilian and commercial sectors.
- **National Registry**: Integration with the Polish national vulnerability notification registry under the KSC Act.
- **Safe-Harbour Status**: Polish Penal Code (Art. 267) penalizes unauthorized access; coordinated disclosure through CSIRT NASK provides evidence of lawful intent.
