# Summary

In Finland, vulnerability disclosure is overseen by the **National Cyber Security Centre Finland (NCSC-FI / Kyberturvallisuuskeskus)**, part of Traficom[^eu-nis2-directive].

# National CVD Framework
- **Designated Coordinator**: NCSC-FI coordinates disclosures across telecom operators, critical sectors, and software vendors.
- **National Vulnerability Intake**: NCSC-FI maintains an active intake and advisories feed, acting as an accredited CVE Numbering Authority (CNA).
- **Safe-Harbour Status**: Finnish law distinguishes between malicious unauthorized penetration and bona-fide security testing conducted within agreed coordinator guidelines.
