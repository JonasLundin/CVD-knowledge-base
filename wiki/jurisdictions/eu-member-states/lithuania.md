# Summary

Lithuania coordinates vulnerability disclosures through the **National Cyber Security Centre (NKSC / NCSC-LT)** under the Ministry of National Defence[^eu-nis2-directive].

# National CVD Framework
- **Designated Coordinator**: NCSC-LT oversees the national CVD process and coordinates with Lithuanian infrastructure providers.
- **Intake Service**: Operates formal intake portals for vulnerability submissions.
- **Safe-Harbour Status**: Lithuanian legislation requires ethical reporting without unauthorized modification of data.
