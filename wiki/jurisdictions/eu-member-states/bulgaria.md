# Summary

In Bulgaria, **CERT Bulgaria** acts as the designated national coordinator for coordinated vulnerability disclosure under the Bulgarian Cybersecurity Act and NIS2 Article 12[^eu-nis2-directive].

# National CVD Framework
- **Designated Coordinator**: CERT Bulgaria, hosted within the Ministry of Electronic Governance.
- **Legal Context**: Transposition of NIS2 Article 12 formalizes the liaison mechanism between discoverers and Bulgarian asset owners.
- **Safe-Harbour Status**: Research gaps exist regarding statutory exemptions in the Bulgarian Penal Code; finders are advised to register via official CERT Bulgaria intake.
