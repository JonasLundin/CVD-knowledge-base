# Summary

Coordinated vulnerability disclosure in Austria is coordinated by **GovCERT Austria** and **CERT.at** pursuant to the Austrian NISG transposing NIS2 Article 12[^eu-nis2-directive].

# National CVD Framework
- **Designated Coordinator**: GovCERT Austria operates as the governmental coordinator, while CERT.at handles national civilian and private sector disclosures.
- **Statutory Foundation**: The Austrian Network and Information System Security Act establishes formal roles for vulnerability intake.
- **Safe-Harbour Status**: Good-faith vulnerability research is guided by Austrian criminal code provisions (§ 118a StGB) with prosecutorial discretion applied when finders act strictly under coordinated disclosure parameters.
