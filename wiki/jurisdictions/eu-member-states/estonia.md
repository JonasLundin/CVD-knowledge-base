# Summary

Estonia manages vulnerability reporting through **CERT-EE**, a department of the Estonian Information System Authority (**RIA**)[^eu-nis2-directive].

# National CVD Framework
- **Designated Coordinator**: CERT-EE acts as the primary contact point for security finders discovering flaws in Estonian public digital infrastructure and private systems.
- **Digital Ecosystem Integration**: Closely tied to the Estonian X-Road and e-identity governance framework.
- **Safe-Harbour Status**: Ethical security research is encouraged provided no unauthorized data extraction occurs; reports submitted to CERT-EE receive prompt administrative triage.
