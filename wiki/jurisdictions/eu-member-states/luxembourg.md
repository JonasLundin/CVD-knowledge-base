# Summary

Luxembourg coordinates disclosures through **GOVCERT.LU** for government systems and **CIRCL** (Computer Incident Response Center Luxembourg) for private and research sectors[^eu-nis2-directive].

# National CVD Framework
- **Designated Coordinator**: GOVCERT.LU / CIRCL. CIRCL is an accredited CVE Numbering Authority (CNA).
- **International Hub**: CIRCL frequently mediates cross-border disclosures across European vendors.
- **Safe-Harbour Status**: Ethical disclosure policies in Luxembourg protect researchers who observe non-destructive testing principles.
