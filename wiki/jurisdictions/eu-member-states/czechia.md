# Summary

The Czech Republic coordinates vulnerability disclosures through **GovCERT.CZ**, housed inside the National Cyber and Information Security Agency (**NÚKIB**)[^eu-nis2-directive].

# National CVD Framework
- **Designated Coordinator**: GovCERT.CZ acts as the trusted coordinator for national infrastructure and vendor disclosures.
- **Policy Guidelines**: NÚKIB maintains active CVD guidelines outlining expectations for bug reports, triage, and patch windows.
- **Safe-Harbour Status**: Ethical disclosure within NÚKIB's recommended protocols is recognized as mitigating public harm under administrative practice.
