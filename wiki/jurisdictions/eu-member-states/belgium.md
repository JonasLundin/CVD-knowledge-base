# Summary

Belgium has enacted one of the European Union's most comprehensive statutory safe-harbour frameworks for coordinated vulnerability disclosure, coordinated by **CERT.be** under the authority of the CCB[^eu-nis2-directive].

# National CVD Framework
- **Designated Coordinator**: CERT.be serves as the central national coordinator under NIS2 Article 12(1).
- **Statutory Safe-Harbour**: The **Royal Decree of 8 February 2023** explicitly exempts ethical hackers and security researchers from computer fraud and unauthorized access prosecution if they notify CCB and follow strict proportionality requirements.
- **Intake Channel**: CCB operates a dedicated online reporting form and secure PGP communication portal.
