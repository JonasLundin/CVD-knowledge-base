# Summary

The Republic of Cyprus designates **CSIRT-CY**, operating under the Digital Security Authority (DSA), as its national CVD coordinator[^eu-nis2-directive].

# National CVD Framework
- **Designated Coordinator**: CSIRT-CY handles vulnerability reporting, triage, and intermediary coordination with Cypriot vendors.
- **Regulatory Oversight**: Managed under DSA regulatory orders aligning with NIS2 Article 12.
- **Safe-Harbour Status**: Formal safe-harbour protection relies on bilateral coordination through CSIRT-CY; standalone research without prior coordination carries legal exposure under Cyprus computer misuse law.
