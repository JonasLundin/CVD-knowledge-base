# Summary

Coordinated vulnerability disclosure in Sweden is coordinated by **CERT-SE**, operating within MSB (transitioning to the Civil Defence Authority)[^eu-nis2-directive].

# National CVD Framework
- **Designated Coordinator**: CERT-SE is the designated CSIRT coordinator pursuant to NIS2 Article 12(1) and the Swedish Cybersecurity Act (SFS 2025:1506).
- **Mandate**: CERT-SE receives vulnerability reports, contacts affected asset owners, and supports coordinated public disclosure.
- **Safe-Harbour Status**: Sweden applies Chapter 4 Section 9c of the Penal Code (dataintrång); researchers operating within CERT-SE's published CVD parameters and without exfiltration are considered to act with consent or justification.
