# Summary

Ireland designates **CSIRT-IE**, situated within the National Cyber Security Centre (**NCSC-IE**), as the national coordinator for vulnerability disclosure[^eu-nis2-directive].

# National CVD Framework
- **Designated Coordinator**: CSIRT-IE facilitates coordinated disclosure for multinational technology firms and critical national entities headquartered in Ireland.
- **Operational Guidelines**: NCSC-IE operates an active CVD program and secure reporting channel.
- **Safe-Harbour Status**: The Irish Criminal Justice (Offences Relating to Information Systems) Act 2017 applies; compliance with NCSC-IE CVD protocols demonstrates lack of unlawful intent.
