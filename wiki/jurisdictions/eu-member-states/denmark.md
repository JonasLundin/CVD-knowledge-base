# Summary

Denmark's national CVD coordinator is **GovCERT**, embedded within the Danish Centre for Cyber Security (**CFCS**) under the Ministry of Defence[^eu-nis2-directive].

# National CVD Framework
- **Designated Coordinator**: CFCS / GovCERT coordinates multi-party disclosures involving Danish critical infrastructure and digital public services.
- **Guidance Framework**: CFCS issues national guidance on responsible disclosure for Danish public sector entities and private companies.
- **Safe-Harbour Status**: Criminal liability under the Danish Penal Code (§ 263) requires absence of authorization; following CFCS CVD parameters provides evidential backing for lack of criminal intent.
