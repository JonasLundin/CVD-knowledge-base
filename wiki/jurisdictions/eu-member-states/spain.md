# Summary

Spain coordinates vulnerability disclosures jointly through **CCN-CERT** (public sector and classified systems) and **INCIBE-CERT** (private sector and citizens)[^eu-nis2-directive].

# National CVD Framework
- **Designated Coordinator**: INCIBE-CERT acts as designated coordinator for civilian industry and is an active CVE Numbering Authority (CNA).
- **Regulatory Framework**: The National Security Framework (ENS) mandates vulnerability handling and reporting across public administrations.
- **Safe-Harbour Status**: INCIBE-CERT's formal CVD guide sets boundaries to safeguard researchers against criminal liability.
