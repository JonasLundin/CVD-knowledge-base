# Summary

Slovenia coordinates vulnerability disclosure through **SI-CERT**, operating within the academic network **ARNES**[^eu-nis2-directive].

# National CVD Framework
- **Designated Coordinator**: SI-CERT is the designated national CSIRT coordinator under NIS2 Article 12(1).
- **Community Engagement**: SI-CERT runs regular awareness programs and operates an accredited CNA for Slovenian software.
- **Safe-Harbour Status**: Disclosures routed through SI-CERT in accordance with ZInfV follow responsible disclosure protocols.
