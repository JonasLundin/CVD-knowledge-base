# Summary

Coordinated vulnerability disclosure in Greece is coordinated by **CSIRT-GR** under the Greek National Cybersecurity Authority[^eu-nis2-directive].

# National CVD Framework
- **Designated Coordinator**: CSIRT-GR coordinates communications between researchers and Greek public/private software owners.
- **NIS2 Implementation**: Law establishing the upgraded National Cybersecurity Authority embeds NIS2 Article 12 CVD duties.
- **Safe-Harbour Status**: Research gaps persist regarding explicit statutory safe-harbour; reliance is placed on CSIRT-GR intermediation.
