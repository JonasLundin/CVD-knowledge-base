# Summary

In Latvia, **CERT.LV** operates as the leading national coordinator for IT security and vulnerability disclosure[^eu-nis2-directive].

# National CVD Framework
- **Designated Coordinator**: CERT.LV, operating under the Institute of Mathematics and Computer Science of the University of Latvia and the Ministry of Defence.
- **Coordinated Disclosure**: CERT.LV runs a public reporting program and acts as mediator for Latvian critical systems.
- **Safe-Harbour Status**: Researchers reporting directly to CERT.LV without malicious extraction are supported under administrative practice.
