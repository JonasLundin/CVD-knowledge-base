# Summary

In Malta, **CSIRT-MT** serves as the national coordinator for coordinated vulnerability disclosure under the Critical Infrastructure Protection Directorate[^eu-nis2-directive].

# National CVD Framework
- **Designated Coordinator**: CSIRT-MT handles notifications and acts as mediator.
- **Regulatory Alignment**: Transposition of NIS2 Article 12 updates the national incident handling architecture.
- **Safe-Harbour Status**: Formal research protections are subject to administrative guidelines; researchers should coordinate directly with CSIRT-MT.
