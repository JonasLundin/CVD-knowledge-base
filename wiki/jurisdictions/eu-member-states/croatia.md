# Summary

Coordinated vulnerability disclosure in Croatia is managed by **CERT.hr**, organized within the Croatian Academic and Research Network (CARNET)[^eu-nis2-directive].

# National CVD Framework
- **Designated Coordinator**: CERT.hr operates as the national designated CSIRT coordinator under Article 12(1).
- **Framework and Policies**: The Cybersecurity Act of 2024 mandates formal coordination channels for ICT products operating within state and critical infrastructure.
- **Safe-Harbour Status**: Finders must follow strict non-exploitation rules to avoid criminal liability under the Croatian Criminal Code.
