# Summary

Norway coordinates vulnerability disclosure through **NorCERT**, embedded within the Norwegian National Security Authority (**NSM**)[^eu-nis2-directive].

# National CVD Framework
- **Designated Coordinator**: NorCERT coordinates disclosures involving Norwegian critical infrastructure and national networks.
- **EEA Alignment**: While not an EU member state, Norway implements NIS2-equivalent principles through updated national cybersecurity legislation.
- **Safe-Harbour Status**: Researchers reporting through NorCERT are protected by established non-prosecution guidelines for responsible testing.
