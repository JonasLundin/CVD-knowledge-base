# Summary

In Liechtenstein, coordinated vulnerability disclosure operates within the EEA framework, with **CERT-FL** acting as the central national Computer Emergency Response Team[^eu-nis2-directive].

# National CVD Framework and Regional Cooperation

As an EEA EFTA Member State, Liechtenstein aligns its cybersecurity governance with relevant internal market legislation. The National Cybersecurity Agency and CERT-FL serve as the technical focal point for receiving vulnerability notices, collaborating closely with neighboring Swiss CSIRTs and the European CSIRTs Network. Researchers reporting vulnerabilities affecting critical entities or public infrastructure benefit from structured coordination channels, while criminal liability provisions under the Liechtenstein Criminal Code require rigorous adherence to non-intrusive discovery principles.
