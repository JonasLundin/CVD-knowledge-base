# Summary

Iceland manages coordinated vulnerability disclosure via **CERT-IS**, the national CSIRT within Fjarskiptastofa[^eu-nis2-directive].

# National CVD Framework
- **Designated Coordinator**: CERT-IS coordinates multi-party disclosures across Iceland's telecom and financial networks.
- **EEA Framework**: Aligned with European standards under the EEA Joint Committee adaptations.
- **Safe-Harbour Status**: Requires adherence to non-destructive testing and direct notification to CERT-IS.
