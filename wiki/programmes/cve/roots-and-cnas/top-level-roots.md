# Summary

**Top-Level Roots (TL-Roots)** occupy the summit of the operational CVE Program hierarchy, delegating authority to Roots and subordinate CNAs[^cve-operational-rules-4-2-0].

# Active Top-Level Roots
1. **MITRE TL-Root**: Oversees commercial software vendors, open-source projects, and general civilian technical domains.
2. **CISA TL-Root**: Oversees industrial control systems (ICS/OT), medical devices, and United States government systems.

# Responsibilities
- Approving new Root appointments.
- Defining global block allocation criteria.
- Adjudicating unresolved inter-Root dispute appeals.
