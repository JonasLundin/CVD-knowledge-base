# Summary

On **November 20, 2025**, ENISA was established as a formal **CVE Root** within the CVE Program[^cve-operational-rules-4-2-0][^enisa-euvd].

# Operational Mandate
1. **European CNA Accreditation**: Mentors, onboards, and supervises European National CSIRTs and regional organizations operating as CNAs.
2. **EUVD Synchronization**: Feeds verified vulnerability data directly into the European Vulnerability Database under NIS2 Article 12(2).
3. **Escalation Intermediary**: Acts as the initial escalation authority for cross-border disclosure disputes involving European CNAs.
