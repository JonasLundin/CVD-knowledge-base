# Programmes

Vulnerability identification and cataloguing programmes: the CVE Program with its rules, record format, services and root structure; the ENISA European vulnerability database; CISA's Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalogue.

## Sections

- [CVE Program](cve/index.md) — The CVE Program: rules, record format, services, roots and CNAs.
- [European vulnerability database](euvd/index.md) — ENISA's EUVD under NIS2 Article 12(2): scope, data model, relationship to CVE and to CRA reporting.
- [Known Exploited Vulnerabilities](kev/index.md) — CISA KEV inclusion criteria, feed, remediation directives.

## Concepts

- [The CVE Program](cve-program.md) — International community program identifying, defining, and cataloging publicly disclosed cybersecurity vulnerabilities.
- [European Vulnerability Database (EUVD)](euvd.md) — Union-wide database maintained by ENISA pursuant to Article 11 of NIS2 collecting, cataloging, and publishing ICT vulnerabilities.
