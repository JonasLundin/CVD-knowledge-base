# Summary

A **Safe Harbour policy** establishes formal legal commitments by system owners not to pursue civil litigation or criminal complaints against security researchers who identify and report vulnerabilities in good faith[^first-cvd-guide].

# Essential Safe Harbour Elements
1. **Authorization**: Explicitly declares good-faith security testing as authorized access under relevant computer fraud and abuse statutes.
2. **Proportionality Boundaries**: Excludes destructive activities, data exfiltration, service disruption (DoS), and social engineering.
3. **Anti-Circumvention Waiver**: Waives claims under copyright anti-circumvention provisions (e.g., DMCA § 1201, EU Software Directive) for testing security safeguards.
