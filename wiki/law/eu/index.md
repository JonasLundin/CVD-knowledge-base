# EU law

CRA Annex I Part II and Article 14 (pointers to CRA-knowledge-base), NIS2 Article 12 CVD framework (pointers to NIS2-knowledge-base), the EUVD legal basis.

## Concepts

- [NIS2 Directive Article 11: Coordinated Vulnerability Disclosure & European Vulnerability Database](nis2-article-11.md) — Statutory framework mandating Member States to establish national CVD frameworks, designate CSIRT coordinators, and establishing the EUVD under ENISA.
- [Cyber Resilience Act: Vulnerability Handling Obligations (Regulation (EU) 2024/2847)](cra-vulnerability-handling.md) — Statutory requirements for manufacturers to maintain CVD policies, patch vulnerabilities without delay, and report actively exploited flaws.
