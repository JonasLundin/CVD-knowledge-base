# Summary

**NIS2 Article 11: Coordinated Vulnerability Disclosure** establishing legal requirements for vulnerability disclosure and handling[^nis2-directive].

Requires Member States to designate CSIRTs as coordinators and mandates ENISA to build the European vulnerability database.

# Legal Architecture
Binding requirements harmonising vulnerability disclosure across the European Union.
