# Law

Legal instruments that require or shape disclosure. EU law is summarised for its disclosure provisions and linked to the sibling bundles for the rest.

## Structure

- [EU law](eu/index.md): CRA Annex I Part II and Article 14 (pointers to CRA-knowledge-base), NIS2 Article 12 CVD framework (pointers to NIS2-knowledge-base), the EUVD legal basis.
- [National law](national/index.md): National CVD frameworks, safe-harbour provisions and computer-misuse exemptions for good-faith research.
