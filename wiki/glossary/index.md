# Glossary

Terms as defined by the CVE Program glossary, ISO/IEC 29147 and 30111, and EU law where they differ.

## Concepts

- [Coordinated Vulnerability Disclosure (CVD)](coordinated-vulnerability-disclosure.md) — Process through which a finder reports a vulnerability to an organization, allowing an agreed coordination window before public release.
- [Proof of Concept (PoC)](proof-of-concept.md) — Code, script, or detailed technical methodology demonstrating the feasibility of exploiting a specific vulnerability.
- [Legal Safe Harbor](safe-harbor.md) — A formal policy or statute ensuring that good-faith security research complying with CVD terms is exempt from anti-hacking civil or criminal liability.
- [Zero-Day Vulnerability](zero-day.md) — A vulnerability unknown to the software vendor or for which no official remediation or patch exists at the time of discovery or exploitation.
