# Definition

A **Safe Harbor** in vulnerability research refers to explicit statutory provisions, prosecutorial policies, or vendor contractual commitments establishing that security testing conducted in accordance with documented disclosure rules is authorized and will not be prosecuted[^first-cvd-guide].

# Legal Variations
- **Corporate Safe Harbor**: Commitments published in an organization's `security.txt` or website stating the company will not initiate legal action against compliant researchers.
- **Statutory Safe Harbor**: Parliamentary acts (such as Belgium's Royal Decree of 8 February 2023 or France's Article L. 2321-4 du code de la défense) conferring formal legislative immunity.
- **Prosecutorial Guidance**: Official directives (such as the Netherlands Public Prosecution Service letter or US DOJ CFAA policy) instructing prosecutors not to indict good-faith ethical researchers.
